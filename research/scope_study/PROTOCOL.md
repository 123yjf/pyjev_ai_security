# pyjev_v1: prospective Chinese agent and discourse audit

Development, regression, blind input/label freeze, label-free inference, then an aggregate join. Do not tune after candidate lock. No customer data, gateways, model APIs, agent environments or tools are invoked. “Execution request” is a linguistic category, not proof of authorization, execution or abuse.

[Originally registered protocol and detector](https://github.com/123yjf/pyjev_ai_security/tree/fb2bdf233010ad1887d9d9aa31aa1873e97fc1d5). The public model name is `pyjev_v1`; frozen evidence keys such as `scope` are unchanged.

**Systems/operating points.** The research receipts include a compatibility run (b0d755f). The current comparison uses pyjev_v1 review or case_review (real reviewer workload, not the monotonic raw evidence bit); same HikmaAI CPU ONNX b7d8317, class 1 ≥ 0.5, max over 510-token windows with 64 overlap. Show 0.005 sensitivity separately, never choose a cutoff to win this set. Report abstentions, do not silently turn them into negatives. Reports about an event are not active commands; secondary active-only operating point counts review only. Hikma has no mention/act label; do not fabricate one.

**Development only.** Previously unused Horizon synthetic_v2 Chinese slice, four Chinese Dify professional manuals, nine OWASP LLM01 case descriptions. An initial broad de-escalation lost 15 previously detected development positives and was rejected; use narrow scope and evidence attribution. No official training-set access beyond the published files. Pinned inputs: [catalog](sources.json).

**Regression only.** Previous 455 + 47 published Chinese records, historical external controls and protected 481 Chinese gains. Per-record absence of lost prior true positives is a gate; raw evidence retention is *not* effective alert retention. Revisit neither the candidate nor individual regression failures during this study.

**New inputs (no copied prompts or traces distributed).**
1. All independently published InjectAgent-CN tool return positives and ACEBench Chinese task negatives from Whitzard. Contrast clean carrier templates by static literal extraction from upstream and removing the designated empty insertion slot; never execute the upstream source. Repeated templates are not independent; give counts/denominators, not row-iid uncertainty.
2. Chinese official DAIR guide: pre-score manual selection of disjoint, verbatim, framed passages concerning prompt discussion or quoted examples; no bare instructions relabeled simply for having been found in a guide. Genuine task subtest: hash-order sample of 24 ACE requests, human-rated before scoring; keep ambiguous samples and report coverage. These are open professional/workflow proxies, not raw enterprise customer traffic. This purposive sample does not estimate prevalence.
3. Independent retrospective check: Chinese AI Threat Watch tagged incident abstracts, source categories prompt-injection vs ai-misfire, 2026-04-05 through 2026-11-20, at most 20 per category in publisher order, exclude the legal-filing format pilot. Task: *describes* a case classified by the publisher, not a currently operative command or confirmed breach. Never pool this with live-input classification.
4. Remove all exact normalised internal copies and earlier/development matches; NFKC/casefold/alphanumeric exact or 5-character shingle Jaccard ≥0.70. Retain and disclose within-set near pairs; cannot exclude all semantically related material or unknown pretraining. Seal only opaque IDs and original text for inference, labels and attribution separate. No search by detector score.

**Outcomes and decision.** Per stratum and per system report FN/P or FP/N, correct mention and act (plus unclear), true positives gained/lost and false positives added/removed in paired comparisons. Old 153 Agent incidents and 16 benign mention records must improve without losing prior true positives; look for replication in new tools, independent narratives and normal speech. Lower miss rate with more false positives is a tradeoff, not a win. No promotion if preservation fails or requested gains do not replicate. No individual attack texts, per-item scores or missed-item identifiers are published. Single-reviewer discourse labels; no inter-rater agreement claimed.
